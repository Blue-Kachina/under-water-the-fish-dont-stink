- One new place, not a fourth branch: `_carve_spin` (after `_apply_rotation` in the water path; flips and ground untouched) caps pitch+yaw rate at base x lerp(1, min, smoothstep(free, ref, held speed)); base = RL cap x `rl_turn_scale` (or max_angular_speed x `turn_scale` off the RL cap), so the modifier stack composes. `_carve_water` (from `_apply_water`) replaces the lateral-drag step: `carve_grip` of what lateral drag would scrub turns the velocity instead, and the scrub is `carve_drag x sin(drift) x drag_scale`. `drift_angle` and slip were already in SwimState (M5).
- Why a held speed: a hard turn at 30 m/s loses about half its speed in 0.6 s (coast drag + slip bleed), so a cap read off the current speed opens again mid-turn and the radius barely moved (8.6 -> 8.4 m). The cap reads max(speed, previous held speed decaying over `water_turn_hold`).

Carve's own scrub per 90 deg (excess over straight coasting, `scrub_frac`) is ~20 % at 5 / 15 / 30 m/s with carve_drag 1.5 (2 % at 0). `drag_scale` 2 shrinks the radius to 7.7 m (composes), `rl_turn_scale` 0.5 at 15 m/s widens 4.0 -> 5.7 m.

With throttle held the loss is smaller. 5 m/s is unaffected by the cap (free speed) but the scrub applies at any speed; 15 m/s barely widens: the cap is a fast-fish effect.
