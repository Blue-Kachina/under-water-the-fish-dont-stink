ROOT CAUSE: (a) the preview fish never moves, so the rig's head-trail is empty at show time and fills only as the pivot-spun head sweeps (2.8 m/s); until the trail is as long as the body (~5.5 s every time the card is shown) the tail is a straight extension whose direction (SpineRig.follow_trail, last two spine points) snaps at every trail append -> whole tail rotates ~0.03 rad per append. (b) the head comes from fish.get_global_transform_interpolated() but the preview moved the fish with the pivot in _process (not a physics tick), so interpolation lags/steps against the 60 Hz tick when frames run faster (144 Hz). Fix: move the fish in _physics_process (as in-match) and pre-roll the trail.
