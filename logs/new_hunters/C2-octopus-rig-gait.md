FINDING: FishController emits `physics_tick(state)` at the start of _integrate_forces, so a rig can add siphon thrust/torque to state.linear_velocity/angular_velocity without touching the controller. Inputs: fish.input_forward/input_reverse/input_stick/input_roll, fish.swim_velocity(), fish.is_boosting.
3. DESIGN: arms are solved in DIRECTION space (model frame): per segment a unit direction state with a spring + follow-the-leader (offset of the previous segment from its target feeds the next), converted to the model's (pitch,yaw,twist) bends through rest bases (shortest arc from the rest direction, so twist is deterministic and cannot pop). FK wave is added to the directions (not to bends) so it is cumulative-correct. Inertial counter-rotation of the state under body rotation makes arms swing wide in turns and air rolls.
4. DESIGN: model has arms AHEAD of the head and the mantle trailing (-Z travel). So the jet bundles the arms forward into a spear (streamlined) on the power stroke, flares them on recovery. Flow drag bends the tips back.
5. DESIGN: logic split: tick(dt) (physics side: jet clock, thrust/torque, floor probe, foot state machine) and step(dt) (visual side: springs, pose). A probe can call step() on a bare rig with a context dict and no fish.
